# Groups and teamwork

Put several bots in one chat, or let them hand work to each other, when a job needs more than one
teammate.

## Start a group chat

Choose **Create** (the **+** at the top of the sidebar), then **New group chat**. Name the group,
pick 2 to 10 bots and choose **Create group**.

Each bot keeps its own memory, computer and instructions; the group only shares the conversation.

## Share a group with other people

**New shared group** invites other accounts by email. The creator and admins can invite people and add their own bots. A member can talk in the group, and can leave it. Only the creator deletes the group or changes who is an admin.

A bot in the group is shown as the owner's name, a hyphen, then the bot's name. A bot from another workspace answers from its own chat, and the text of that reply is posted back here. It does not bring its owner's other chats or private memory with it.

@ in a shared group lists the people and the bots who are in it. Naming a person leaves their name in the message. A bot there starts only when the message names it, or uses @everyone. A message that names no bot stays in the chat, and no bot starts.

A private group is unchanged: it still belongs to one person and holds 2 to 10 of that person's bots.

## Who answers

- In a private group, with no mention, the first bot answers and can bring in the others.
- **@Name** wakes that bot. Mention several to wake them all.
- **@everyone** wakes every bot in the group.
- In a shared group, a bot starts only when the message names it or uses @everyone.

To rename the group or change who is in it, open **Group settings** from the group's header.
**Delete group** removes the group chat.

## Bots asking other bots

A bot can message another of your bots directly, for example to ask your research bot a question.
The chat shows **Messaged** or **Message from** with the other bot's name. Click it to read the
exchange; it is view-only.

Bots decide whom to ask from each bot's **How other bots see it** description in its settings
under **Advanced**. Keep it to one line about what the bot is for.

Your bots can message each other. **Allow cross-space bot communication**, under **Permissions** in
a bot's settings, applies when an account still belongs to more than one workspace.

## Subagents

For a big task, a bot can split off short-lived helpers that work in parallel and report back. Each
appears as a card in the chat showing **Running**, **Completed** or **Failed**. Subagents finish with
the task; they have no chat or memory of their own.

## Bots that build bots

A bot can create new bots, and with **Manage other bots** turned on it can also organize and
configure your bots. See [Bots that manage bots](./fleet-management.md).
