# Workspace

You have one workspace. Your bots, chats, files, memory, and computer all live in it.

## What lives here

- Chats, files, and [memory](./memory.md).
- The Team Computer and every bot's [computer](./computer.md).
- [App connections](./integrations.md).
- [Saved credentials](./bot-secrets.md).
- Skills a bot saves.

Your account settings apply here: [models](./models.md), [voice](./voice-and-calls.md),
[approvals](./approvals.md), and your credentials vault.

**Allow cross-space bot communication**, under **Permissions** in a bot's settings, applies when an
account still belongs to more than one workspace.
