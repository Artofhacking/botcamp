# Getting started

BotCamp gives you persistent AI bots. Each one has its own conversations, memory, routines, and
computer, and keeps working on the web, desktop, and mobile apps.

## Create your first bot

1. Sign in at [botcamp.demox.site](https://botcamp.demox.site). To run BotCamp on your own computer,
   use the [desktop app](./desktop-app.md).
2. Tell the bot its job in plain words. It asks for anything else it needs.

More in [Bots and chats](./bots-and-chats.md).

## Connect your apps

Choose **Connect apps** and connect **QQ邮箱** when you want that mailbox saved on the account. See
[Connect apps](./integrations.md).

## Give it a schedule

Ask for a routine, such as "every weekday at 8am, summarize my inbox". The bot runs it on its own
computer and reports back in the same chat. See [Routines](./routines.md).

## Decide what it can do alone

Choose whether bots ask before acting in [Approvals](./approvals.md).

## Bring an agent you already have

Coming from OpenClaw, Hermes or another assistant? [Import it](./import-an-agent.md) as a new bot.
